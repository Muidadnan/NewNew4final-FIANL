## Part B — Domain description

**Domain.** Australian supermarket shopping, within the Shopping & Consumer Life domain. The story examines Woolworths and Coles' physical store footprints, historical national grocery-sales concentration, and nominal food-retailing turnover.

**Who and why.** The intended audience is an Australian shopper without specialist knowledge of statistics. The story explains why the most stores does not necessarily mean the most stores per resident, how the two chains' state-level footprints differ, and why store counts, historical sales share and nominal food-retailing turnover should not be treated as interchangeable measures.

**What.** The visualisation combines September–October 2026 Woolworths and Coles state-level location snapshots from ScrapeHero; ABS March 2026 state and territory population estimates; ACCC estimates of national take-home food and grocery sales shares for FY2002–03, FY2006–07 and FY2022–23; and ABS seasonally adjusted monthly food-retailing turnover to June 2025. Derived measures include combined outlet totals, stores per 100,000 residents, jurisdiction-level chain proportions, rankings, retailer–jurisdiction treemap leaves, percentage-point changes, year-on-year growth and a rounded 100-cell waffle. No finer-grained store-location or consumer records are implied.

**How.** Four map views organise the geographic chapter: a choropleth encodes population-adjusted outlet rates through geographic polygons and colour; a proportional-symbol map overlays area-scaled circles for absolute outlet totals on real state outlines; a schematic circle cartogram replaces land shapes with circles scaled by absolute store counts; and an equal-area tile-grid map compares Woolworths' share of the two chains' outlets using uniform tiles. These views distinguish rates, raw totals, geographic context and relative retailer share. Grouped bars, connected dots, a population scatterplot, a nested retailer-first treemap and parallel coordinates compare the chains and their distribution. A waffle chart, bump chart and diverging bars explain historical grocery-sales shares and shifts. A line chart and year-by-month heatmap communicate nominal food-retailing turnover and year-on-year variation. Tooltips, clear legends, concise findings and source notes support an accessible four-chapter narrative.

**AI acknowledgement.** OpenAI ChatGPT assisted with website layout and HTML/CSS, Vega/Vega-Lite specifications, data transformations, debugging and drafting or editing explanatory text. The public data sources, dates and interpretation limitations are acknowledged on the website.
