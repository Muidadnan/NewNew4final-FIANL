A STATE-LEVEL FOOTPRINT

Where are the two chains?

Location totals tell one story; locations per resident tell another. Four complementary maps reveal the Woolworths and Coles presence across six states and two mainland territories—not every supermarket in Australia.

THE DESIGN QUESTION: ABSOLUTE OR RELATIVE?

NSW has more outlets overall, but Tasmania has more per resident. Both facts can be true. First, compare stores per resident on a choropleth. Next, place circles sized by total outlets on real geography. Then replace land area with a circle cartogram to expose the difference between geography and magnitude. Finally, use a dot-density map to split those totals into approximately 20-store units for each retailer.

THE CONCLUSION / WHAT WE CAN SAY

Australia has concentrated supermarket sales and a changing food-retailing total, but these charts cannot establish prices, local access or market power on their own. Compare like with like: geographic presence, sales share and turnover answer different questions.

HOW TO READ THE EVIDENCE

A small glossary for a big grocery story.

These definitions explain the decisions behind the charts. The point is to understand the data, not memorise technical vocabulary.

Absolute vs relative

A total counts locations. A per-capita rate divides the total by population: stores ÷ residents × 100,000. Both are useful, but answer different questions.

Area, colour & position

The larger a proportional circle or treemap rectangle, the more stores it represents. A sequential light-to-dark colour scale encodes smaller to larger numeric values.

Store share vs sales share

Store share counts a retailer’s locations out of the two sampled chains. Sales share estimates the portion of all take-home grocery dollars associated with a participant.

Percentage vs percentage points

A rise from 5% to 8% is +3 percentage points, not +3% growth. The diverging bars use percentage points.

A map is not a travel-time model

State rates and approximate symbols do not tell us how far a shopper lives from a store, whether it has accessible transport, or what the food costs there.

Read the exact value

Hover on a chart to see figures. On the charts with clickable legends, select a retailer or jurisdiction to highlight it; click again or reset the legend to return to the broader view.
