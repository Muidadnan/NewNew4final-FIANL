# Provenance and caveats — DV2 supermarket showcase

1. ScrapeHero, *Number of Woolworths Supermarkets locations in Australia in 2026*, snapshot 13 July 2026, state-by-state count: https://www.scrapehero.com/location-reports/Woolworths%20Supermarkets-Australia/ . The dataset represents one reporting company's estimates, not an authoritative census.
2. ScrapeHero, *Number of Coles Supermarkets locations in Australia in 2026*, snapshot 3 August 2026, state-by-state count: https://www.scrapehero.com/location-reports/Coles%20Supermarkets-Australia/ . Excludes other Coles Group stores.
3. Australian Bureau of Statistics, *National, state and territory population, March 2026*: https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/population/national-state-and-territory-population/mar-2026 . For per-capita store comparisons, population dates do not exactly match store-count snapshots. All state populations here use ABS March 2026 rounded values, not ScrapeHero population denominators.
4. ACCC, *Supermarkets inquiry 2024–25 interim report*, Figure 1.1, estimate of national take-home food and grocery sales in FY2002–03, FY2006–07 and FY2022–23: https://www.accc.gov.au/system/files/supermarkets-inquiry-2024-2025-interim-report.pdf . CAUTION: figures are estimates of share of **national take-home food and grocery sales** not market shares of store numbers. Metcash category covers wholesale supply to independent supermarkets. Do not interpret it as one retail chain. Other retailers is an aggregate. Numeric totals may differ from 100 slightly due rounding.
5. Australian Bureau of Statistics, *Retail Trade, Australia, June 2025*, Food retailing monthly seasonal adjusted turnover series: https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/industry/retail-and-wholesale-trade/retail-trade-australia/jun-2025 . This includes ALL FOOD RETAILING (supermarkets, liquor and specialised food stores), not grocery supermarket sales alone. Units are AUD $ million, current prices, seasonally adjusted. Nominal sales changes are not the same as quantities purchased or inflation-adjusted spending. ABS ceased this publication following June 2025.
6. Genuine state boundary geometry: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tonywr71/GeoJson-Data/master/australian-states.json . The source is attributed in the website footer.

**Scope**: Two large supermarket chains only for the geography and location count charts. Locations outside these chains (ALDI, IGA etc) are omitted; do not interpret counts as all Australian supermarkets. The four map views are a geographic choropleth (chart 01), a geographic proportional-symbol map (chart 02), a schematic circle cartogram (chart 03) and a dot-density map (chart 04). The proportional-symbol map preserves geographic context while the cartogram deliberately replaces it. Cartogram and dot-density positions are illustrative and approximate and do not encode distance or land area.

**AI disclosure**: Chart code, narrative, layout and derived CSV files were assisted by OpenAI ChatGPT, with an additional chart, stronger explanations and static audits on 28 October 2026. Underlying measured values sourced as above; store totals are sourced; schematic dot positions are presentation-only and are not actual observations. 

**Additional transformation details (13 Oct 2026):** Population denominators were updated to ABS March 2026 state population estimates, released 17 September 2026 (thousands: NSW 8670.1, VIC 7154.2, QLD 5739.5, WA 3096.2, SA 1916.8, TAS 580.7, NT 268.9, ACT 489.7). Density = published location count / published population × 100,000. Dorling cartogram circles are sized by combined location counts and laid out in schematic geographic order. Waffle illustration rounds 57.3% to 57 whole one-percentage-point tiles, which is disclosed in the caption. This is not fictitious observational data.

**Revision 28 Oct 2026 — corrected treemap:** Chart 08 now uses the sourced 2026 retailer-by-state store counts, with hierarchy `All stores → retailer → state/territory`. Cartogram pairwise circle distances were tested for non-overlap; choropleth values and population-normalised denominators were recalculated; all 100 waffle cells and 14 JSON files were checked. **Presentation-only fields (28 Oct 2026 update):** `labelPopM` and `labelStores` in `states.csv` are hand-positioned *label coordinates*, not population or store-count observations. Chart 07 uses them solely to move direct labels away from clustered markers; the plotted circle positions still use the sourced values. Chart 09 uses independent min–max normalisation solely for visual comparison and presents actual raw values via tooltips and the axis-range key. Chart 03 uses manually prepared schematic centres, with small-jurisdiction annotations outside their tiny circles. These changes do not fabricate additional records or finer-geography observations.

**Market definition cross-check (ACCC Figure 1.1, p. 9 of the interim report):** 57.3% is combined 32.5% + 24.8% national take-home food and grocery sales for FY2022–23. This is distinct from the ACCC's approximately 67% for FY2023 national *supermarket retail sales*, with a narrower denominator. The website explains the difference explicitly.
